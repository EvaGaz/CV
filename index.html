Dr. Eva Gazagne
Behavioral Ecologist & Primatology Researcher

I am a postdoctoral researcher in primatology and behavioral ecology, with expertise in thermal drone surveys, primate monitoring, ecosystem services, and long-term field research across Southeast Asia and Africa. My work integrates ecological theory, conservation practice, and non-invasive monitoring technologies to support evidence-based biodiversity management.

Research Capabilities Portfolio

Integrated field-to-analysis workflow for primate ecology research across Southeast Asia and Africa.

Thermal drone monitoring of primate communities
Design and deployment of UAV/TIR protocols for detection, census support, and sleeping-site analyses in dense tropical habitats.
Habituation of primate populations
Behaviorally informed habituation frameworks to reduce observer bias while protecting animal welfare and data quality in long-term studies.
Forest structure & phenological monitoring
Vegetation and phenology tracking to link habitat quality, fruit availability, and primate ranging or foraging decisions.
Eco-ethological data collection
Standardized behavioral and ecological sampling pipelines combining focal follows, habitat variables, and disturbance context.
Camera trapping & bioacoustic monitoring
Multi-method population monitoring using camera traps, passive acoustic methods, and ground surveys for occupancy and detectability insights.
Biostatistical analysis
Advanced statistical workflows in R for detection modeling, movement ecology, habitat-use inference, and comparative primate analyses.
GIS & geospatial analyses
Spatial modeling and mapping of primate distributions, survey coverage, and conservation-priority landscapes for decision support.
